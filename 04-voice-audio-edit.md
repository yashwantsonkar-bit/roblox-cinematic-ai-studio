# Voice, audio and edit

1. Assign a stable voice identity to each character.
2. Generate dialogue/narration using a connected, authorized provider.
3. Generate or source licensed/original SFX and music.
4. Verify audio files exist and duration is plausible.
5. Assemble shot clips in story order with FFmpeg or a configured editor.
6. Mix dialogue clearly above music; avoid clipping.
7. Add captions when requested or useful.
8. Verify final export plays and has expected resolution, duration and audio.
